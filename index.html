Hanul Jung 정하늘

Senior Researcher  ·  Mobility AI Convergence Research Section
Daegu-Gyeongbuk Research Division, ETRI, Daegu, Republic of Korea

My research addresses data-driven motion control and system identification for high-performance mechatronic systems, with an emphasis on control-relevant identification — building models that are accurate where the closed loop actually needs them. This formulation has been extended to multi-axis stages with asymmetric actuation and to plants subject to unmodeled, operating-condition-dependent disturbances.

My broader motivation is that data-driven control occupies the interface between model-based synthesis and machine learning, preserving the interpretability and stability guarantees of the former while admitting the representational capacity of the latter. I am currently extending this perspective to multi-axis field robotics, where lightly damped manipulators and mobile manipulation platforms must attenuate structural vibration and sustain motion performance under varying payloads and unstructured operating conditions.

Interests: Data-driven control · System identification · Disturbance observer · MIMO precision motion control · Mobile manipulation · Industrial embedded systems
