I am a Senior Researcher at ETRI, working on motion control for robotic and mechatronic systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from DGIST, where I was advised by Prof. Sehoon Oh, and spent a year as a visiting researcher in the Control Systems Technology group at TU/e with Prof. Tom Oomen. My research focus is on data-driven motion control and control-relevant system identification — building models that are accurate where the closed loop actually needs them, and extending that principle to field robotics platforms operating under varying payloads.

Interests: Data-driven control · System identification · Disturbance observer · MIMO precision motion control · Mobile manipulation · Industrial embedded systems
